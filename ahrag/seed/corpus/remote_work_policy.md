# Hybrid and Remote Working Policy (v3.0)

Policy code: **POL-HR-021**

## 1. Principle

Northwind Systems operates a hybrid model. Roles are designated office-anchored,
hybrid, or remote-first by the department head, and the designation is recorded
against the role, not the individual.

## 2. Attendance expectation

Hybrid roles are expected on site for a minimum of **two days per week**,
averaged over a calendar month. There is no fixed anchor day at organisation
level; teams set their own anchor days and publish them in the team charter.

Remote-first roles have no minimum attendance expectation but are expected to
attend quarterly team gatherings and the annual company meeting.

## 3. Equipment and expenses

Employees receive a one-off home-office allowance of £450 on joining, and a
£150 refresh allowance every three years. Travel to a designated home office
location is not reimbursable for hybrid or remote-first employees. Travel to a
non-home office at the employer's request is reimbursable under the Expenses
Policy.

## 4. Working from abroad

Working from outside the country of employment requires prior approval and is
capped at **20 working days per calendar year** for tax and permanent
establishment reasons. Requests are approved by the department head and
recorded by the HR Business Partner. Approval is not granted retrospectively.

## 5. Relationship to other policies

This policy does not alter annual leave entitlement, which is governed by the
Annual Leave and Time-Off Policy (POL-HR-014). Days worked from abroad are
working days, not leave days.

## 6. Review

This policy is reviewed annually by the HR Director. The next scheduled review
is January 2027.
