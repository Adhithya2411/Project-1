EXPENSES AND TRAVEL POLICY (v1.2)
Policy code: POL-FIN-008
Applies to: all employees and approved contingent workers

1. GENERAL PRINCIPLE

Employees are reimbursed for reasonable expenses necessarily incurred in the
performance of their duties. Reimbursement requires a valid receipt and a
business justification recorded in the expense claim.

2. SUBMISSION DEADLINE

Claims must be submitted within 60 days of the date the expense was incurred.
Claims submitted after 60 days require director-level approval and may be
refused. Claims submitted after 120 days are not reimbursable in any
circumstance.

3. TRAVEL

Standard class rail and economy class air travel are the default. Business class
air travel is permitted only for flights with a scheduled duration exceeding
six hours, and requires prior approval from the department head.

Mileage for use of a private vehicle is reimbursed at 45p per mile for the first
10,000 miles in a tax year, and 25p per mile thereafter.

4. ACCOMMODATION

Accommodation is capped at 180 GBP per night in London and 130 GBP per night
elsewhere in the United Kingdom. International caps are published in the
finance intranet pages and are reviewed twice yearly.

5. MEALS

Subsistence is reimbursed at actual cost up to 30 GBP per day when travelling
overnight on business. Alcohol is not reimbursable. Client entertainment
requires prior approval and must record the names and organisations of those
entertained.

6. NON-REIMBURSABLE ITEMS

Commuting to a designated home office location, parking fines, personal
subscriptions, and travel insurance purchased separately from the corporate
policy are not reimbursable.

7. ESCALATION

Disputed claims are escalated to the Finance Business Partner for the claiming
department. Persistent policy breaches are referred to HR under the disciplinary
procedure.
