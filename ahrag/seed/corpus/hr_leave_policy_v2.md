# Annual Leave and Time-Off Policy (v2.1)

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy sets out the annual leave entitlement, carry-over rules, and
approval process for all permanent employees of Northwind Systems. It replaces
the 2023 edition in full. Contractors and interns are covered by separate
arrangements described in the Contingent Worker Handbook.

## 2. Annual leave entitlement

Permanent full-time employees accrue **26 days** of paid annual leave per
calendar year, accruing at 2.166 days per completed month of service. Part-time
employees accrue pro rata against contracted hours.

The entitlement was increased from 22 days to 26 days as part of the 2025
compensation review. The increase applies to leave accrued on or after
1 January 2025 and is not applied retrospectively to leave accrued in 2024.

## 3. Carry-over

Employees may carry over a maximum of **10 days** of unused annual leave into
the following calendar year. Carried-over days expire on 31 March. Any balance
above 10 days is forfeited at year end unless a written exception is approved by
the employee's manager and the HR Business Partner before 15 December.

This is a change from the previous policy, which permitted carry-over of 5 days
with a 31 January expiry.

## 4. Requesting leave

Leave requests are submitted through the Workday self-service portal. Requests
of five consecutive days or fewer require line-manager approval only. Requests
longer than five consecutive days require line-manager approval and notification
to the HR Business Partner at least 21 calendar days in advance.

Managers must respond to a leave request within five working days. An
unanswered request is not an approved request; employees must not treat silence
as approval.

## 5. Sick leave interaction

Sickness occurring during a booked period of annual leave may be reclassified as
sick leave where a fit note covering the period is supplied within 7 days of
return. Reclassified days are returned to the annual leave balance.

## 6. Leaving the organisation

On termination, accrued but untaken leave is paid in lieu at the employee's base
daily rate. Leave taken in excess of accrual is recovered from the final salary
payment. The calculation uses the accrual position on the last working day.

## 7. Exceptions and escalation

Exceptions to this policy require joint approval from the HR Director and the
relevant department head. Escalations should be raised through the HR service
desk, referencing policy code **POL-HR-014**.
