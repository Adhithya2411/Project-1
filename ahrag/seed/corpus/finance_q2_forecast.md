# RESTRICTED — Q3 2026 Revenue Forecast and Margin Review

Classification: Restricted — Finance only. Do not redistribute.

## 1. Headline forecast

Q3 2026 net revenue is forecast at **£48.2m**, against a plan of £51.0m. The
£2.8m shortfall is driven by slower enterprise renewals in the EMEA segment and
a one-off credit note related to the Atlas reconciliation defects.

## 2. Gross margin

Forecast gross margin is **71.4%**, down 180 basis points against plan. The
decline is attributable to increased acquirer processing fees following the
temporary failover to the secondary acquirer during the Atlas cutover.

## 3. Headcount and compensation

The 2026 compensation review increased the annual leave entitlement from 22 to
26 days. The accrual impact of the additional four days is £1.1m across the
group, recognised in the period in which leave accrues.

Engineering headcount is forecast to grow from 214 to 231 by Q4, weighted
towards the Platform Engineering rota expansion.

## 4. Contingency

A £3.0m contingency remains unallocated. Finance recommends holding £2.0m
against the Atlas legacy decommission slip and releasing £1.0m at the Q3 close.

## 5. Distribution

This document is distributed to the CFO, the finance leadership team, and the
audit committee only. Requests for access are handled by the Finance Director.
