# Annual Leave and Time-Off Policy (v1.4)

> Superseded edition. Retained for reference and for queries about leave
> accrued before 1 January 2025.

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy sets out the annual leave entitlement, carry-over rules, and
approval process for all permanent employees of Northwind Systems.

## 2. Annual leave entitlement

Permanent full-time employees accrue **22 days** of paid annual leave per
calendar year, accruing at 1.833 days per completed month of service. Part-time
employees accrue pro rata against contracted hours.

## 3. Carry-over

Employees may carry over a maximum of **5 days** of unused annual leave into the
following calendar year. Carried-over days expire on 31 January. Any balance
above 5 days is forfeited at year end.

## 4. Requesting leave

Leave requests are submitted through the Workday self-service portal. Requests
of ten consecutive days or fewer require line-manager approval only. Requests
longer than ten consecutive days require line-manager approval and notification
to the HR Business Partner at least 14 calendar days in advance.

Managers must respond to a leave request within ten working days.

## 5. Leaving the organisation

On termination, accrued but untaken leave is paid in lieu at the employee's base
daily rate.

## 6. Exceptions and escalation

Exceptions to this policy require approval from the HR Director. Escalations
should be raised through the HR service desk, referencing policy code
**POL-HR-014**.
