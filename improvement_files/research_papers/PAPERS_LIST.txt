================================================================================
1. BASELINE PAPERS (Compete Against These)
================================================================================

ADAPTIVE-RAG (Jeong et al., NAACL 2024)
  Title: "Adaptive-RAG: Learning to Adapt Retrieval-Augmented Large Language
          Models through Question Complexity"
  PDF:   https://aclanthology.org/2024.naacl-long.389.pdf
  Code:  https://github.com/starsuzi/Adaptive-RAG  [CLONED → baseline_code/]
  Key:   Uses T5-large classifier trained on query complexity labels to route
         between no-retrieval, single-step, and iterative retrieval. This is
         the system your project's B5 baseline is modeled after. You need to
         properly reproduce this or compare against their published results.

SELF-RAG (Asai et al., ICLR 2024 — Oral, top 1%)
  Title: "Self-RAG: Learning to Retrieve, Generate, and Critique through
          Self-Reflection"
  PDF:   https://openreview.net/pdf?id=hSyW5go0v8
  Code:  https://github.com/AkariAsai/self-rag  [CLONED → baseline_code/]
  Key:   Trains a single LM to emit special "reflection tokens" that control
         when to retrieve and evaluate generation quality. Strong baseline
         for adaptive retrieval.

CORRECTIVE RAG (Yan et al., 2024)
  Title: "Corrective Retrieval Augmented Generation"
  PDF:   https://arxiv.org/pdf/2401.15884.pdf
  Code:  https://github.com/HuskyInSalt/CRAG  [CLONED → baseline_code/]
  Key:   Adds a retrieval evaluator that assesses document quality and triggers
         web search for low-confidence retrievals.

HOW TO DOWNLOAD THE PDFs
================================================================================
Most PDFs can be downloaded directly from the arxiv/ACL links above.
Use your browser or run:

  Invoke-WebRequest -Uri "URL" -OutFile "research_papers/filename.pdf"

Example:
  Invoke-WebRequest -Uri "https://arxiv.org/pdf/2005.11401.pdf" `
    -OutFile "improvement_files/research_papers/RAG_lewis_2020.pdf"
