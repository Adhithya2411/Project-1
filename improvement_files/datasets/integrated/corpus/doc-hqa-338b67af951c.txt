A Bachelor of Design (B.Des.  or B.Design) degree is usually an undergraduate academic degree awarded for a course or major that generally lasts three or four years.  It is an arts-centered degree that is a counterpart to the Bachelor of Fine Arts (BFA) degree.  Bachelor of Design degrees have been popular in Canada and Australia for several decades and are becoming increasingly popular in the United States as discipline-specific design education expands and becomes more specialized.  There are several variants of the Bachelor of Design degree, including the Bachelor of Design Arts and the Bachelor of Design Studies.
