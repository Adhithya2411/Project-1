Fial ("Fabbrica Italiana Automobili Legnano"), direct translation "Italian Automobile Factory Legnano", was a company that manufactured industrial and marine engines in Legnano.  In 1906 it entered the automobile business and manufactured one car, Legnano Type A 6/8 HP.  The car had a two-cylinder 1135 cc engine.  Two years later in 1908, the company was placed in liquidation after filing for bankruptcy.  In 1909 the company was taken over by Rosa & Ferrario and car production ended.  The 6/8 model was the only model they ever produced.  The Legnano is at display on Museo Nazionale dell'Automobile in Turin.
