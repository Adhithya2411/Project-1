Traeger Park (currently known under naming rights as TIO Traeger Park) is a sports complex located in Alice Springs, Northern Territory, Australia, and is named after Alfred Hermann Traeger.  The park was officially opened by Anne Catherine Smallwood (nee Traeger) Alfred's younger daughter.  The primary stadium in the complex caters for Australian rules football and cricket and has a capacity of 10,000.  The complex also has a small baseball stadium.
