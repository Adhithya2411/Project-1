The 1951 Arizona State Sun Devils football team was an American football team that represented Arizona State College (later renamed Arizona State University) in the Border Intercollegiate Athletic Association (BIAA) during the 1951 college football season.  In their first season under head coach Larry Siemering, the Sun Devils compiled a 6–3–1 record (3–1 against BIAA opponents) and outscored their opponents by a combined total of 308 to 176.  The Sun Devils finished 4-2 at home and 2-1-1 on the road.  Harold Brown and Bob White were assistant coaches.  All home games were played at Goodwin Stadium in Tempe, Arizona.  Arizona State's team captain was quarterback Harold Self.
