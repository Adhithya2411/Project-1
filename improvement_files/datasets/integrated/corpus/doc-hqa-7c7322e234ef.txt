The 1973 Queen's Club Championships was a tennis tournament played on grass courts at the Queen's Club in London in the United Kingdom.  The men's tournament was part of the 1973 Commercial Union Assurance Grand Prix circuit while the women's event was part of the 1973 Women's Grand Prix tour.  It was the 74th edition of the tournament and was held from June 17 through June 23, 1973.  Ilie Năstase and Olga Morozova won the singles titles.
