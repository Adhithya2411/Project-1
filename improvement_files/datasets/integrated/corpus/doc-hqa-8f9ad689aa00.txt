The Honda Quint was a subcompact car manufactured by Honda in Japan from 1980 to 1985.  It was introduced in February 1980 in Japan as a five-door liftback version of the Honda Civic, being more upscale than the Civic, and was sold at the "Honda Verno" sales channel in Japan.  The Quint was made available to export markets including Europe and Southeast Asia in 1981, with the export name being Honda Quintet.  Beginning in 1983, this model was also sold in Australia as the Rover Quintet.  The Quint was succeeded by the Honda Quint Integra in 1985.
