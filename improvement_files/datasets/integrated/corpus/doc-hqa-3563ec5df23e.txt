"Slow Jamz" is a single by American rapper Twista featuring Kanye West and Jamie Foxx.  It was released in late 2003 as the lead single from his album "Kamikaze" and the second single from Kanye West's debut album "The College Dropout".  The Kanye West version includes an intro and two extra verses by Jamie Foxx, and excludes the original outro by Twista.
