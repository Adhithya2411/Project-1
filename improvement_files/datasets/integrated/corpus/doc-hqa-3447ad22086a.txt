Kevin J. O'Connor serves as general counsel at Point72 Asset Management.  Previously, he served as an attorney appointed by President George W. Bush and was unanimously confirmed by the United States Senate as Connecticut’s 48th United States Attorney in 2002.  From January to April 2006, O'Connor served as Associate Deputy Attorney General of the United States.  In 2007, O'Connor served as Chief of Staff to United States Attorney General Alberto R. Gonzales.  In 2008, O'Connor was unanimously confirmed as Associate Attorney General of the United States, the number three position at the U.S. Department of Justice (DOJ), a post he held until 2009, when he left the DOJ to join the law firm of Bracewell and Giuliani.
