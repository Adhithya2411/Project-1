The 68th annual Venice International Film Festival was held in Venice, Italy between 31 August and 10 September 2011.  American film director Darren Aronofsky was announced as the Head of the Jury.  American actor and film director Al Pacino was presented with the Glory to the Film-maker award on 4 September, prior to the premiere of his upcoming film "Wilde Salomé".  Marco Bellocchio was awarded with the Golden Lion for Lifetime Achievement in September.  The festival opened with the American film "The Ides of March", directed by George Clooney.
