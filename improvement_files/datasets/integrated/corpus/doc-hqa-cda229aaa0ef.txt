The Golden Voyage of Sinbad is a 1973 fantasy film directed by Gordon Hessler and featuring stop motion effects by Ray Harryhausen.  It is the second of three Sinbad films released by Colombia Pictures, the others being "The 7th Voyage of Sinbad" (1958) and "Sinbad and the Eye of the Tiger" (1977).  The film stars John Phillip Law, Tom Baker, Takis Emmanuel, and Caroline Munro.  It won the first Saturn Award for Best Fantasy Film.
