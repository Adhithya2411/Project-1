Gabrielle-Suzanne Barbot de Villeneuve (28 November 1685 – 29 December 1755) was a French author influenced by Madame d'Aulnoy, Charles Perrault, and various précieuse writers.  Barbot is particularly noted for her original story of "La Belle et la Bête", which is the oldest known variant of the fairy tale "Beauty and the Beast".
