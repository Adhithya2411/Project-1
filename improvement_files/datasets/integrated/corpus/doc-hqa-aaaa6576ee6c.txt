The 2015 Nepal Premier League (Everest Premier League) was supposed to be the second edition of the Nepal Premier League.  Also known as Wai Wai Nepal Premier League, the Twenty20 format was supposed to be held in 2015 in Kathmandu.  Initially it was scheduled to be held from 26 March to 4 April 2015 but Cricket Association of Nepal insisted that it will not support NPL unless its name is changed.  Later the board decided to support NPL and provided the ground for the competition.  The name has been changed into Everest Premier League and is re-scheduled from 24 September to 3 October 2016.
