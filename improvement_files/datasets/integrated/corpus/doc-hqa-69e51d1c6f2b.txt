"Dub-I-Dub" is a song recorded by Danish Eurodance act Me & My.  It was released in September 1995 as the first single from the eponymous album.  The song was successful in many European countries, being a top ten hit in Denmark (number 1), Sweden where it peaked at number 2, and Belgium.  The song was also a top 20 hit in Finland, Switzerland, Norway and Austria.  The single was the subject of multiple remixes and was included on band's best of "The Ultimate Collection", released in 2007.  "Dub-I-Dub" was used in video games, such as "Dance Dance Revolution 2ndMix", "Dancing Stage EuroMix", "Dance Dance Revolution Party Collection", and "Dance Dance Revolution X".
