Bhadrabas is a village development committee in Kathmandu District in the Bagmati Zone of central Nepal, located approximately 15 km northeast of Kathmandu.  At the time of the 2011 Nepal census it had a population of 2,388 and had 503 houses in it.  The oldest high school in the eastern Kathmandu Valley, Adarsh Uchha Madhayamik Vidhalaya is located in Bhadrabas.  The Nepal Red Cross Society is active in Bhadrabas and it has notable health centre.  In 1984, a tobacco smoking World Health Organization questionnaire was given to the inhabitants of Bhadrabas to survey smoking trends in the country.
