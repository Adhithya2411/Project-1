Mat Dan (born Daniel Tyler, 1990) is an adventurer and host born from England and lived in Malaysia since 2009.  He first settled in Malaysia after his adventures to Kapas Island.  He also converted to Islam and adopted the name Kareef Daniel Abdullah.  He is known for his eloquence speaking Terengganu dialect pretty well.  Now, he is better known in the TV show about his adventures with his friends entitled Haramain Backpackers - Trans Siberian.
