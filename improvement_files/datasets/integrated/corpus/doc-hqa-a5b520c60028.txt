Pakdam Pakdai, also known internationally as Rat-A-Tat, is an Indian animated comedy TV series airing formerly on Nickelodeon India and currently airing in Sonic Nickelodeon.  It is inspired by "Oggy and the Cockroaches", a French animated series based on the silent and friendly war between a cat, Oggy, and three cockroaches.  The show began in April 2013.
