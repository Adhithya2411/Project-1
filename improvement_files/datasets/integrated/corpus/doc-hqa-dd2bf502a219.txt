The 1958 Arizona State Sun Devils football team was an American football team that represented Arizona State University in the Border Intercollegiate Athletic Association (BIAA) during the 1958 college football season.  In their first season under head coach Frank Kush, the Sun Devils compiled a 7–3 record (3–1 against BIAA opponents) and outscored their opponents by a combined total of 271 to 131.  Assistant Coaches were Cecil Coleman, Chuck Fairbanks, Paul Kemp, & Dick Tamburo.  Arizona State's team captains were QB John Hangartner and RT Tom Ford.  The Sun Devils finished (5-2) at home and (2-1) at home.  One home game was played at Goodwin Stadium and six home games were played at Sun Devil Stadium in Tempe, Arizona.
