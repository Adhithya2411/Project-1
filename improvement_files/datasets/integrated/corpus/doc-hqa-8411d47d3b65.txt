Dianchi Lake (Chinese: 滇池 , "Diānchí"), also known as Lake Dian and Kunming Lake (昆明湖 , "Kūnmínghú"), is a large lake located on the Yunnan-Guizhou Plateau close to Kunming, Yunnan, in southern China.  Its nickname is the "Sparkling Pearl Embedded in a Highland" and it was the model for the Kunming Lake in the Summer Palace in Beijing.  Its name is the source of Yunnan's Chinese abbreviation 滇 .
