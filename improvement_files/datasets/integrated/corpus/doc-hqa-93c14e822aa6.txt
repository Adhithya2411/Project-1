The St. Nicholas Roman Catholic Cathedral (Ukrainian: Костел Св.  Миколая ; translit. : "Kostel Sviatoho Mykolaia") is the second Roman Catholic cathedral built in Kiev, the capital of Ukraine.  Today the building is no longer a cathedral and is shared between the Roman Catholic Church of Ukraine and the National House of Organ and Chamber Music.  Another Catholic church, the St. Alexander Roman Catholic Cathedral, is the oldest Catholic church in the city and is located near the European Square behind the Ukrainian House in Kiev.
