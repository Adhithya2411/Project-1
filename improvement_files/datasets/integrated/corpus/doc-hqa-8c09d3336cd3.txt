Brico Dépôt (] ) is a French chain of DIY and Home Improvement stores, headquartered in Longpont-sur-Orge.  The chain was created in 1993 by Castorama Dubois Investment, Castorama Dubois was later purchased by the UK Kingfisher Group.  There are over 100 stores in France.  There are also 16 in Spain, 3 in Portugal and 5 in Poland.  .  Some (15) were also opened in Romania after the acquisition of the Bricostore chain by Kingfisher.
