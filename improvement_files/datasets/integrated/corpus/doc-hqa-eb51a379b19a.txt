The 1952 Arizona State Sun Devils football team was an American football team that represented Arizona State College (later renamed Arizona State University) in the Border Intercollegiate Athletic Association (BIAA) during the 1952 college football season.  In their first season under head coach Clyde B. Smith, the Sun Devils compiled a 6–3 record (4–0 against BIAA opponents) and outscored their opponents by a combined total of 247 to 121.  Assistant Coaches were Harold Brown and Bob White.  All home games were played at Goodwin Stadium in Tempe, Arizona.  The Sun Devils finished (4-3) at home and (2-0) on the road.  ASC finished in 1st place of the Border Conference in 1952.  Arizona State's team captain was C Virgil Savage.
