Dayna Hart is an American actress, model and television host born in New York City, who is also the founder of the Give & Get Non-profit organization.  Hart made her on air debut back in 2008 on several morning talk shows and covered the White House Correspondents Association Dinner in 2008 and 2009.  In 2012, she made her big screen debut starring opposite Isaiah Washington and Vivica A. Fox in the indie film Doctor Bello.
