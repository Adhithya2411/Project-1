Royston Macauley "Mac" Evans (13 January 1884 – 12 March 1977) was an Australian sportsman.  He played both cricket and soccer for Western Australia.  As a cricketer, Evans played 11 first-class matches for the Western Australian state team between 1907 and 1924.  Playing as an all-rounder, Evans made 270 runs at an average of 15.00, and took one wicket, at an average of 214.00.  He captained the side against the MCC in October 1924.  He also played cricket for the North Perth Cricket Club in the WACA District competition.
