Spare Me is a 1992 "bowling noir" film that, as one film reviewer wrote, "twists conventions of the Western and thriller genres within its parallel universe of bowling monomania."  Matthew Harrison's feature film directorial debut, the 16mm feature was made for less than $80,000 US and won awards at the Rome Film Festival, New Orleans Film Festival, Long Island Film Festival and others.  Harrison used the cash award from a prize at the Avignon Film Festival to make his second feature, the Sundance Jury Prize winning "Rhythm Thief".
