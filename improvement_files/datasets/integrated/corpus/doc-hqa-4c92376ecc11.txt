Hans Jenisch (19 October 1913 – 29 April 1982) was a "Kapitänleutnant" in Nazi Germany's "Kriegsmarine" during World War II and a "Kapitän zur See" in West Germany's "Bundesmarine".  He commanded the Type VIIA U-boat "U-32" , sinking seventeen ships on seven patrols, for a total of  gross register tons (GRT) of Allied shipping, to become the 26th highest scoring U-Boat ace of World War II.
