The FPS Personnel and Organisation (Dutch: "FOD Personeel en Organisatie" , French: "SPF Personnel et Organisation" , German: "FÖD Personal und Organisation" ), also referred to as the FPS P&O, was a Federal Public Service of Belgium.  It was created by Royal Order on May 11, 2001, as part of the plans of the Verhofstadt I Government to modernise the federal administration.  It is a so-called horizontal Federal Public Service because it isn't responsible for a specific policy field, but provides services to the other Federal Public Services.
