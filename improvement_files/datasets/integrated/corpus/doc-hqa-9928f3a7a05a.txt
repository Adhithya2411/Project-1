Cardinal Newman Catholic School is a Roman Catholic secondary school that caters for pupils aged between 11 and 18, located in the Warden Hills area of Bedfordshire, England.  Opened in September 1968, the current head is Mr Richardson, with the deputy heads being Fleur Musonda and Lucy Whelan.  There are currently over 1500 students on roll.  The school is named after a cardinal of the Roman Catholic Church, John Henry Newman.
