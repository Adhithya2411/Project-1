Margaretha Murray (stage name Milan Murray) (30 October 1974) is a South African actress.  She is most well known to the South African public for roles in various soap operas, but she has also played and starred in feature films, and regularly does stage performances.  In addition she co-anchors "Ontbytsake", a weekly Breakfast show on kykNET, an Afrikaans channel on DStv.  She lives in Johannesburg with her husband, Schalk van der Merwe, her son Steph and daughter Lua.  She was raised in the Cape Province, South Africa.
