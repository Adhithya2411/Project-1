Madge Jenison (1874–1960) was an American author, activist, and bookstore owner.  She wrote novels, short stories, cultural criticism, and scripts.  Her father, Edward Spencer Jenison, was a prominent Chicago architect who helped rebuild the city after The Great Chicago Fire of 1871.  Her sister, Nancy Blanche Jenison, was a pioneering woman physician.  In the early years of the 20th century Madge Jenison moved from Chicago to New York, where she found success with writing, primarily for magazines.
