The Kripalu Center for Yoga & Health is a non-profit organization that operates a health and yoga retreat in Stockbridge, Massachusetts.  Its 160000 ft2 facility is a former Jesuit novitiate and juniorate seminary built in 1957.  The center has described itself as North America's largest residential facility for holistic health and education.  With 2013 revenue of $34.7 million, it employed about 626 people as of 2008 and can accommodate more than 650 overnight guests.
