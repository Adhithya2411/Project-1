William Street (Irish: "Sráid Liam" ) is one of the main thoroughfares of central Limerick City, Ireland.  The street starts at a junction with O'Connell Street (Limerick's main thoroughfare) and continues in a south-east direction.  The street is named after William Pery, 1st Baron Glentworth a relation of Edmund Sexton Pery who is credited with the development of the present day Limerick City Centre area known as Newtown Pery.
