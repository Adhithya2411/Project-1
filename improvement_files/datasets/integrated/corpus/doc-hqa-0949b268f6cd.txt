Wilton Mall at Saratoga (or simply The Wilton Mall) is a regional shopping center, located off Interstate 87 exit 15 in the town of Wilton, directly north of Saratoga Springs, New York.  The mall has a gross leasable area of 763270 sqft .  The mall is anchored by Bon-Ton, Dick's Sporting Goods, JCPenney, HomeGoods, and Sears; in addition, it features a food court and movie theater.  It is currently owned by Macerich, having been purchased from Wilmorite Properties of Rochester, New York in 2004.
