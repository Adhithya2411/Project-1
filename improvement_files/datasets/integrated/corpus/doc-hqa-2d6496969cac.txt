The 1955 Arizona State Sun Devils football team was an American football team that represented Arizona State College (later renamed Arizona State University) in the Border Intercollegiate Athletic Association (BIAA) during the 1955 college football season.  In their first season under head coach Dan Devine, the Sun Devils compiled an 8–2–1 record (3–1 against BIAA opponents) and outscored their opponents by a combined total of 343 to 107.  Assistant Coaches were Robert Carey, Tom Fletcher, Frank Kush, & Al Onofrio.  Arizona State's team captains were FB Bob Sedlar & LT John Jankans.  The Sun Devils finished (5-1) at home and (3-1-1) on the road.  All home games were played at Goodwin Stadium in Tempe, Arizona.
