J D Wetherspoon plc, branded as Wetherspoon's, is a pub company in the United Kingdom and the Republic of Ireland.  Founded in 1979 by Tim Martin, the company owns just under 1,000 outlets, including the chain of Lloyds No.1 bars and the chain of Wetherspoon hotels.  With its headquarters in Watford, Wetherspoons is known for converting unconventional premises into pubs.  The company is listed on the London Stock Exchange and is a constituent of the FTSE 250 Index.
