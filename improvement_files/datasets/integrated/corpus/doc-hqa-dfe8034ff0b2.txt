Mossbourne Victoria Park Academy is a school in Victoria Park, Hackney (London, UK).  It is the second school to be named under Mossbourne Academy.  The school was created in 2014: the building formerly used by Cardinal Pole Roman Catholic School was first built to drawings by Robert Lewis Roumieu for the French Hospital (La Providence), which opened there in 1865.  They have produced year 7's,8's,9's and 10's.
