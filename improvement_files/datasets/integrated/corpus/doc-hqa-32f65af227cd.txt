Under the Blue Moon is the fifth studio album by American R&B boy band New Edition by MCA on November 24, 1986.  Their fourth album and third with MCA and first album after New Edition member Bobby Brown was voted out of the group, the group was going through a transitional phase during this period.  It is their only album as a quartet.  The album reached number 43 on the "Billboard" 200, and number 18 on the R&B albums chart.  It was later certified gold by the Recording Industry Association of America (RIAA).
