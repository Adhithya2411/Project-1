The second season of "Parks and Recreation" originally aired in the United States on the NBC television network starting September 17, 2009, and ended on May 20, 2010.  The season was produced by Deedle-Dee Productions and Universal Media Studios, and series co-creators Greg Daniels and Michael Schur served as executive producers.  Like the first season, it focused on Leslie Knope (Amy Poehler) and her staff on the parks and recreation department of the fictional Indiana town of Pawnee.  The episodes were approximately 22 minutes long each, all of which aired at 8:30 p.m. on Thursdays.
