Christopher Oscar Peña is a playwright, screenwright, actor and educator who resides in New York City.  He teaches theater at NYU's Tisch School of Arts and is a member of the Playwrights Unit at Goodman Theatre in Chicago.  He also has had work commissioned by Yale Repertory Theatre.  Peña is the co-writer of the Golden Globe nominated TV series, Jane the Virgin and was on the "Future Broadway Power List," by Backstage (magazine) in 2014.
