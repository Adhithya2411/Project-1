Umar Akmal (Urdu: ‎ ; born 26 May 1990) is a Pakistani cricketer.  He made his ODI debut on 1 August 2009 against Sri Lanka and made his Test debut against New Zealand on 23 November 2009.  He is a right-handed batsman and a part-time spinner.  Like his two brothers, Adnan and Kamran, Umar has kept wicket for the national team any many ODIs.  His wife name is noor Fatima.
