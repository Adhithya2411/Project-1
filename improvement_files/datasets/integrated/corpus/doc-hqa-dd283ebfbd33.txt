Booty Luv (originally called Booty Luv and Cherise & Nadia), were a British dance group formed in June 2006 via the Hed Kandi label.  The group comprises two R&B vocalists, Cherise Roberts and Nadia Shepherd, both of whom were in the original line-up of the hip hop and R&B group Big Brovaz.  To date, they have released one BPI Silver-certified studio solo album as a twosome, and have had five top twenty hits in the UK.  They have also achieved international success, gaining hits in Ireland, Poland, the Netherlands and Germany.
