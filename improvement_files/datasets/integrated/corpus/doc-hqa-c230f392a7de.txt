Trevor Kennedy (born Trevor John Kennedy; born in Perth, Western Australia, on 24 June 1942) is an Australian businessman and company director.  He has served on the board of directors of many Australian companies, including Consolidated Press Holdings and Qantas.  He is a former journalist and right-hand man of Kerry Packer and a former business associate of Malcolm Turnbull, current leader of the Australian Parliamentary Liberal Party and the 29th Prime Minister of Australia.
