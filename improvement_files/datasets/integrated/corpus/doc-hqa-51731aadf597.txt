Ganzhou Huangjin Airport (former) () was the old airport serving the city of Ganzhou in Jiangxi Province, China.  The airport was located in Shuinan New Area in Zhanggong District.  It was named after the nearby village of Huangjin.  Originally built in 1936, it was one of the first civil airports in China, but was closed in March 2008 when all services were transferred to the new airport, also named Ganzhou Huangjin Airport.
