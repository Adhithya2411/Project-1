Van Staphorst v. Maryland, 2 U.S. (2 Dall.)  401 (1791), was the first case docketed with the United States Supreme Court.  Although the court agreed to hear and decide the case, the suit was settled before oral arguments. " Collet v. Collet" was the first appellate case docketed with the court. " West v. Barnes" was the first case decided by the court.
