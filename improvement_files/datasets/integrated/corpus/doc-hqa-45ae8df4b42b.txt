Morning Joe is a weekday NBC News morning news and talk show, airing from 6:00 a.m. to 9:00 a.m. Eastern Time on the network's cable news channel MSNBC.  It features former GOP representative Joe Scarborough providing both enterprise reporting and discussion on the news of the day in a panel format with co-hosts Mika Brzezinski and Willie Geist.  It was created as the replacement for "Imus in the Morning," which was cancelled in April 2007 after simulcasting on MSNBC since 1996.
