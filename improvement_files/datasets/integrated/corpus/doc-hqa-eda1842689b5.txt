Amar, después de amar (English: "To love after love") is a 2017 Argentine telenovela produced by Telefe Contenidos and broadcast by Telefe premiered on 23 January 2017.  Starring Mariano Martínez, Isabel Macedo, Eleonora Wexler, Federico Amador, Michel Noher, Manuela Pal, Delfina Chaves, Franco Masini, Camila Mateos, Manuel Ramos and Virginia Lago in the lead roles.
